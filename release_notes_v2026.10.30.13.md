# AutoCommand III — v2026.10.30.13

Shell polish for the Shields Up look.

## What changed

- **Hamburger menu.** The left sidebar now collapses: a ☰ item at the top of the strip toggles between full labels (grouped WATCH / CONTAIN / SYSTEM) and icons-only — the same collapsed-pane behavior as Shields Up's navigation. The toggle never navigates; your current tab stays put.
- **Flat blue buttons in Light.** The diagonal two-blue gradient that primary buttons and selected combo items wore read heavy on the light surfaces. Light now uses Shields Up's flat accent fills (#007AFF primary, #D70015 danger); Dark keeps its subtle gradients.
- Light remains the default (adopted once on upgrade since v2026.10.30.12; Settings → theme still switches live, and an explicit Dark choice sticks forever).

No other changes from v2026.10.30.12.
